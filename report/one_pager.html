Paddy Burnt-Area IntelligenceProduct one-pager · Sangrur, Punjab

What fire counts miss in Punjab's paddy fields

A five-day straw map for buyers of paddy straw, from free Sentinel-2 data

1

Problem

Official farm-fire data count satellite fire detections, and iFOREST's 2025 report says satellites miss most stubble fires Reported: via The Tribune. In 2025 every daytime VIIRS detection in Sangrur came between 12:28 and 14:39 IST Ours, while most large Punjab farm fires burned after 3 pm Reported. Counts show neither how much burned nor where straw is still lying.

2

User

The procurement manager of a biogas plant that runs on paddy straw, such as Verbio's at Lehragaga in Sangrur, which uses 100,000 t of residue a year by the company's account Reported. The decision: where to send balers before straw is burnt. Second user: the district agriculture officer, who needs to know whether burning is really falling Recommendation.

3

Satellite data

Sentinel-2 L2A20 m with both SWIR bands; a view every 2–3 days in 2025 Ours. The core.
VIIRS active fires375 m fire pixels from NASA FIRMS: the counts under test.
Landsat 8/9 (HLS)30 m views that settled doubtful reference points.

All free and open. Radar (Sentinel-1) is the next test, for the smoky weeks Recommendation.

4

Analysis

Every 20 m pixel is followed through eighteen 5-day composites: green, then harvested, then burnt (NBR, MIRBI and NIR tests). The map was checked at 240 reference points (F1 0.92), matched against fire detections, rerun for 2021, and set beside a weekly fire forecast for 2026. A Random Forest only tied the rule, so the simpler rule stays Ours.

6

Decision

For the buyer: every five days, the 1 km squares holding fields harvested in the last ten days and not yet burnt, ranked by straw, so balers go first where the most straw is lying. At the late-October peak that was about 185,000 ha Ours.

For the department: measure burnt area directly, a few days behind; fire counts can no longer show whether burning is falling Recommendation.
